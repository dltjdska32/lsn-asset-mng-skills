# Alternative Asset Analysis

1. Identify the exposure form: spot asset, exchange product, fund, derivative, or physical holding.
2. Use asset-appropriate drivers such as supply, flows, real rates, currency, custody, venue, and liquidity.
3. Verify price timestamp, venue, quote currency, and freshness through the runtime.
4. Separate protocol or physical-market facts from market narratives and news claims.
5. Evaluate custody, counterparty, regulatory, premium/discount, and exit risks as applicable.
6. Never apply corporate earnings or discounted-cash-flow valuation to Bitcoin, gold, or silver.
7. Report uncertainty and unavailable physical premiums or venue data explicitly.
