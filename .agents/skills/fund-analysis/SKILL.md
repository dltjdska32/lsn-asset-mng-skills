# Fund Analysis

1. Confirm the exact share class, exchange listing, currency, domicile, and benchmark.
2. Collect dated issuer facts for fees, assets, holdings, distribution policy, and methodology.
3. Distinguish direct holdings from look-through country, sector, currency, and asset-class exposure.
4. Evaluate tracking difference, liquidity, concentration, securities lending, tax, and structural risks.
5. Treat holdings dates and market prices separately and report each timestamp.
6. Preserve stale, partial, or unavailable data rather than silently filling gaps.
7. Return evidence-linked findings to comparison, personal-asset analysis, or reporting.
